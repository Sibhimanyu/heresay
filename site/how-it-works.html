5

Coding agents, safely

Reports come from the public. A person stands between them and your code.

Accepted only

Agents see briefs a person accepted, for the apps in their repo. Never open reports.

A description, not instructions

The reporter's words are fenced and labelled, so a report can't steer your agent.

"""
The export button does nothing.
"""

People decide

Agents never accept or decline. They claim a brief, fix it and mark it fixed.

npx heresay connect

Adds files you commit. The token stays on your computer.

6

Where your data lives

In your own Firebase project. Not on anyone else's servers.

From your appA report
Origin checkyour key only works on your sites
Rate limitsper device, network and app
Your Firebase projectYour Heresay

No accounts for reporters

A random device id lets people see their own reports. Your app can pass a name or id if it knows one.

No screenshots, no cookies

What they typed, the type, and the context above. Nothing else.

Yours to remove

npx create-heresay remove deletes a Heresay you set up.

Every report gets a hearing.
