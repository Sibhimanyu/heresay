# The fix note

The person who reported the problem reads this in the app, next to "Fixed". They are a user,
not a developer.

- One or two sentences. Say what changed, in the words they used.
- Describe what they will see, not what you did in the code.
- No file names, function names, ticket numbers or jargon.
- If they need to do something (update the app, reload), say so.
- Don't promise dates. Don't blame anyone.

Good: "The Export button now downloads a CSV of your notes. Reload the page if it doesn't show up."
Good: "We moved Cancel plan to the top of Settings, so it's easier to find."
Not: "Fixed null check in exportHandler (PR #214)."
