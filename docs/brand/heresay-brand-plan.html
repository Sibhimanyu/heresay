Your call

Which mark?
Seal, Ear or Bell
I'd pick A, the Seal: it carries "on the record", and it works at favicon size.
Court words for your users too?
I'd use them in your dashboard only. Your users see plain words, plus "Heard".
Trademark check
Heresay sounds identical to Hearsay (Yext's mark), and sound counts in trademark law. Get it checked before launch.
