02 · The idea

Every report gets a hearing.

Heresay puts a Report button in any app. Users say what is wrong, the developer rules on it, and the user hears back. The brand is built on that one promise.

Here, say it.

Read the name one way: an invitation. Here is the place. Say it.

hearsay → firsthand

Hear it the other way: hearsay is secondhand talk. Heresay is the opposite, straight from the person using the app.

The mark is a quote with a face. The quotation marks are eyes, looking up at whoever is speaking.

03 · Construction

One circle, six waves, two quotes.

Everything is measured from R, the radius of the base circle. The waves rise and fall by R/8. The eyes are the brand quotation mark, drawn at 1.25 and tilted 24° upward.

RBase circle radius · 74 units in a 200 box
Waves6 around the circle, depth ± R/8
EyesTwo quotation marks, scale 1.25, tilted −24°, centres at (80, 106) and (124, 101)
GlanceRight eye sits 5 units higher: the face looks up and slightly right

05 · Clear space and size

Give it room to breathe.

Keep clear space of at least one eye-width (x) on every side. Nothing enters it: no text, no edges, no other marks.

Minimum size

48 px
32 px
24 px
16 px

Screen: 16 px. Print: 6 mm. Below 24 px, use the small-size artwork, which is drawn larger inside its tile so the eyes stay open.

12 · Voice

Plain first, pun second.

We sound like a helpful person, not a courtroom. The legal wordplay is a wink, used where it fits exactly and never at a user's expense.

Say

"Here, say it."

"Heard. We'll get back to you."

"Declined: it's under Settings → Plan."

"Every report gets a hearing."

Don't

A pun in an error message

Jokes inside a decline

"Your objection is inadmissible"

Urgency theatre: "CRITICAL!!"

13 · Misuse

Please don't.

The mark only works as drawn. Each of these breaks the face, the colour or the promise.

✕ Don't stretch it

✕ Don't rotate it

✕ Don't recolour it red

✕ Don't outline it

✕ Don't remove the eyes

✕ Don't swap the eyes

✕ Don't add effects

✕ Don't use low contrast
